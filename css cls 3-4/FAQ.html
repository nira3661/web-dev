Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product, billing, and our services. Can’t find what you're looking for? Reach out below.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You get full, unrestricted access to all Pro tier features for 14 days without requiring a credit card upfront. If you choose not to subscribe before your trial ends, your workspace seamlessly downgrades to our free tier with zero data loss.

Can I switch plans or cancel at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription straight from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, and downgrades apply at the conclusion of your ongoing billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), along with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. For annual Enterprise plans, we can also generate custom purchase orders and facilitate ACH bank transfers.

How secure is my data and cloud storage?

Security is built into every layer of our platform. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 encryption. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and conduct routine third-party penetration audits.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits and students?

Yes! We offer a 50% lifetime discount for registered non-profit organizations, as well as complimentary student packs for accredited academic institutions. Simply submit a proof of eligibility via our contact form to get set up.

Can I invite guests or external collaborators?

Unlimited guest viewers can be invited to view read-only assets at no additional cost. If you need external contractors with editing permissions, you can provision individual single-seat licenses with restricted project access.
