My Love Language Is Poetry

The way I journal is defensive—like a trial where I put myself on the stand, presenting reasons to justify my feelings in front of a hostile crowd. Poetry is different; it’s where you get to hide. I write to leave behind proof of myself, so that on days I question my existence, there is evidence a heartless person could never have written this.
